# Visual Bible

Main subject: a single ancient-style female fan performer in pale layered robes with long weighted sleeves.

Location: moonlit ceremonial terrace with stone floor, banners, and light wind.

Prop lock: folding fan with dark ribs and pale silk leaf. It opens, closes, snaps, slices, and rotates in hand. Do not morph it into a sword, ribbon, umbrella, or second fan.

Movement system: body-driven fan logic. Fan trails follow wrist rotation, elbow path, torso torque, foot placement, and sleeve drag. Laban qualities move from glide and float to slash and dab accents, then return to bound control.

Avoid: extra characters, broken wrists, extra limbs, prop mutation, random cuts, unreadable action, and a loose final pose.
