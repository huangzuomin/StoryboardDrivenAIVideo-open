# Control Strategy

- Primary control strategy: hybrid.
- Segment control mode: rhythm_performance_board.
- Segment execution mode: single_continuous_shot.
- Layout pattern: 12_panel_rhythm_sheet.

This fixture uses a compact fan-kata sequence to test production handoff rather than image quality. The storyboard controls beat order, camera staging, action path, timing, and composition. Character, prop, environment, style, and clean keyframe references are separate asset roles.

High-motion control notes:

- Body-driven prop logic: the fan arc is driven by wrist rotation, shoulder line, torso torque, footwork, sleeve drag, and the final weight drop.
- Laban movement qualities: glide, float, slash, dab accent, flow, and bound control.
- Do not skip, merge, reorder, or reinterpret panels.
- Final payoff: a steady held fan seal with cloth settling after the explosive burst.
